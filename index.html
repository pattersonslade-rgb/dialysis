Dialysis Log

A free, simple tracker for people on dialysis and the people who help them. Log sessions (date, weight before and after, blood pressure, UF removed, notes) and drinks against the daily limit you enter.

  • No account, no sign-up
  • Works offline after the first visit
  • Big buttons and large text
  • Your data stays on your phone; backup and spreadsheet export are free
  • No subscription. Optional Pro is a one-time $4.99

Not medical advice. It only records the numbers you enter. Always follow your care team.
