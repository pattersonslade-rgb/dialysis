Dialysis Log: privacy & disclaimer

Not medical advice

Dialysis Log only records numbers you type in. It does not give medical, diet or fluid advice, and it does not judge whether any number is good or bad. Your fluid limit is whatever you enter. Always follow your nephrologist, dialysis nurse and care team. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers. Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in your browser's storage, and is never uploaded. After the first load the app needs no internet, with one exception: if you enter a Pro license key, the app sends that key (and nothing else) to Gumroad, the store that sold it, to check it, and quietly re-checks it about once a month while online.

Backups and CSV exports are files you save yourself. Clearing this site's data in your browser, or deleting the home-screen app on some phones, can erase your entries, so save a backup regularly.
